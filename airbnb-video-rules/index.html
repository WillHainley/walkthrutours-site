Can you put a video on an Airbnb listing?

No. As of October 2026 the Airbnb listing editor takes photos only. There is no upload field for a video file and no way to embed a YouTube or Vimeo player, and links in the description are not allowed. Hosts ask us about this more than anything else, so here is where a film does work and what each place wants.

Where video works

PlaceTakes video?What it wants
VrboYes, on the listingVertical 9:16, under 60 seconds, 1080p, no added music. A person reviews it before it goes live.
Booking.comNoPhoto gallery only.
Your own booking siteYesAnything. A wide 16:9 file near the top of the page is the biggest lift, and you keep the platform fee.

Instagram Reels, TikTok, FacebookYesVertical for Reels and TikTok, square or wide for Facebook.
Airbnb guest messagesYes, as a linkSend the film after a guest books or asks a question. It cuts the back and forth about layout.

What that means for one film

One film has to come as three cuts or you end up re-editing it for every place. We send every film as a wide cut for your site and YouTube, a vertical cut under 60 seconds with no music for Vrbo and Reels, and a square cut for Facebook. The same rooms in the same order, just framed for each screen.

What you do about Airbnb

Put the film everywhere Airbnb is not. The film then lands in two places that matter. Guests who find the place on Instagram or Google and then book direct, and guests already on Airbnb who get the film in the first message and stop asking whether the bunk room has a door.

What we make it from

The photos already on your listing. Nothing to schedule and nobody comes to the property. Most films are back the next day, and you pay once per property, from $75.
