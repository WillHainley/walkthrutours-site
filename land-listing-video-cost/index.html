What a land listing video costs in 2026

A video on a land listing used to mean hiring a drone pilot. Now there are four ways to get one, and they range from $9 a month to $1,200 a tract. Here is what each one costs and what you actually receive.

The four options

Option2026 priceWhat you get
Local drone pilot$200 to $400 for undeveloped land in Texas, $100 to $250 each at volumeA flight on one day in one season, aerial stills, a flyover with the boundary drawn on. Someone has to drive out. Extra tracts, terrain views and rush delivery are usually add ons of $75 to $150 each.
Satellite flyover studio$500 to $1,200A 3D flyover built on Google imagery with the boundary and nearby landmarks labeled, sometimes narrated. No flight, but a week or more of turnaround and a fee if you change the map later.
Self serve 3D map software$9 to $199 a monthAn automated terrain flyover and boundary overlay you make yourself. Fast and cheap, but it is a map, not a film, and nobody checks it.
Parcel film from public records$99 a listing here, $50 for your first tractA 25 to 30 second flyover built from county imagery and USGS elevation data, with the boundary, acreage, creek length, road frontage, relief and flood zone on screen, sourced. Three cuts, an unbranded copy for the MLS, and an interactive parcel page.

What buyers actually want to see

Land buyers ask the same five questions on every tract. Where exactly is the line, how much of it floods, is there water, how much rise is there, and what is the frontage. A drone flight shows the view. A parcel film answers the five questions, because the answers come from the public record, not from the camera.

Why ours is $99

There is no flight, no travel and no site visit. The terrain data and county imagery are public, so the cost is our time to build the film and check every frame. The first tract is $50 so a broker can judge it on a listing of their own. Five tracts for a brokerage are $350, any counties.

Turnaround

Usually within two days of a reply. The film is often already built for listings we reach out about, in which case it is same day.
